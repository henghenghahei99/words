attacking adj.攻击的 heart attack心脏病发作 under attack在攻击下
attack sb with sth用某物攻击某人 make an attack on...对...发起攻击
2. faithful adj.忠诚的 faithfully adv.忠诚地=in good faith faith n.信任；忠实
faithless adj.不忠诚的 faithlessly adv.不忠诚地
be faithful to.... 对...忠诚 have/lose faith in sb对某人有/失去信心
keep faith with sb对某人守信用
3. hold v.抱着；拿着；保持；容纳；拥有；举行（过去式/过去分词held-held)
holder n.持有者 hold on to sth抓住不放 get/take hold of抓住
hold on不挂断电话，等一下=hang on=hold the line
hold a meeting举行会议 hold back抑制 hold sth out递给
4.responsible adj.负责任的；可信任的 responsibility n.责任=duty
irresponsible adj.不负责任的 responsibly adv.负责地 response n.反应
respond v.回应 be responsible for sth=take responsibility for sth 对某事负责
have the responsibility for doing/n. 有做某事的责任
be responsible to sb对某人负责 sense of responsibility责任感
5.awake adj.醒着的（反义词asleep:睡着的） keep/stay/remain awake保持清醒的
v.使醒来（过去式/过去分词awoke-awoken) awake from... 从...醒来
awaken v.使醒来
wake v.醒来（过去式/过去分词woke-woken)
wake up醒来（代词需放中间，名词可放中间或后面）
Eg:wake me up wake up his sister/wake his sister up
7. flat n.公寓（复数+s）=apartment adj.平坦的=level
flatly adv.平坦地 flatness n.平坦 flatten v.变平
8. choice n.选择（复数+s） choose v.选择（过去式/过去分词chose-chosen)
have no choice but to do sth=can do nothing but do sth别无选择只能...
make a choice做选择=make choices choose (sb) to do sth选择（某人）做某事
choose sb as...选某人当... chooser n.选择者
choose from... 从...中选择 as you choose随你的便
9. advise v.建议；劝告=suggest advice n.建议（不可数）=suggestion（可数）
a piece of advice=a suggestion一条建议
advise sb to do sth建议某人做某事
advise/suggest doing 建议做某事
give sb some advice/suggestions on/about how to do sth
给某人一些关于怎么做某事的建议
ask sb for advice向某人征求意见 take/follow one’s advice听取某人的意见
10. cause v.引起；造成；导致=lead to=result in n.原因=reason
cause sb to do sth导致某人做某事
the cause of... ...的起因=the reason for...
11. complain v.抱怨；投诉 complaint n.抱怨；投诉 complaining adj.抱怨的
complain to sb向某人抱怨/投诉=make a complaint to sb
complain about sth/sb抱怨/投诉某事/某人=make a complaint about sth/sb
complain to sb about sth/sb向某人抱怨/投诉某事/某人
a letter of complaint一封投诉信
12. litter n.垃圾（不可数）=rubbish=garbage v.丢垃圾
litter bin=rubbish bin=trash bin垃圾箱
No litter.不许乱丢垃圾。（此时litter是名词）
No littering!（此时litter是动词）=Don’t litter.禁止乱丢垃圾。
(No+doing表示禁止做某事）
litter on the floor在地板上乱扔垃圾
13. unlikely adj.不大可能发生的=impossible adv.未必
likely adj.可能的=possible/probable
adv.大概；有可能=possibly/probably/maybe/perhaps
be likely/unlikely to do sth可能/不可能做某事
It is likely/unlikely that+句子 可能/不可能...
like v.喜欢（反义词dislike:不喜欢） prep.像 （反义词unlike:不像）
like/love/enjoy doing sth喜欢做某事 dislike doing sth不喜欢做某事
look like看起来像 sound like听起来像
14. relieve v.方便，接手；缓解；救济 relief n.救济；缓解
relieve oneself大小便 relieve from... 从...中缓解
15. indoors adv.在室内 indoor adj.室内的
stay indoors待在室内 indoor sports室内运动
outdoors adv.在室外 outdoor adj.室外的
go outdoors去室外 outdoor activities室外活动
16. magical adj.有魔力的 magic n.魔术 magician n.魔术师
magically adv.用魔法地 a magic show魔术表演
magic tricks魔术 magic wand魔杖
17. wealth n.财富=richness wealthy adj.富有的=rich wealthily adv.富有地
the wealthy/rich富人 be wealthy/rich in... 在...方面富有
a wealth of+不可数名词/可数名词复数 大量...
18. queen n.女王；王后 king n.国王 prince n.王子 princess n.公主
19. servant n.仆人（复数+s) public servant公务员
service n.服务 serve v.服务；担任
after sale service售后服务 customer service客户服务
serve sb sth=serve sth to sb为某人提供某物
serve as+职位/身份 担任某种职务 serve the people服务人民
20. pride n.自豪 v.以...自豪 proud adj.自豪的；骄傲的 proudly adv.自豪地
take pride in=be proud of 为...感到自豪/骄傲 national pride民族自豪感
pride oneself on sth 以...自豪
Eg:She prides herself on her success.她以她的成功为傲。
21. among prep.在...中（三者及三者以上） between prep.在...之间（两者）
22. relationship n.关系 relation n.关系 relate v.有联系 related adj.有联系的
relative n.亲戚 have a relationship with sb/sth与某人/某物有关系
the relationship between...and .... ...跟...之间的关系
develop/maintain a relationship发展/维持关系 in relation to关于...（介词短语）
relate A to B 将A与B关联 be related to... 与...有关（动词短语）
23. grow up长大 grow v.成长；种植；逐渐变得（过去式/过去分词grew-grown）
growth n.生长 grow flowers种花 grow dark天逐渐变黑
grow old变老 grow into长成... grow to do sth逐渐开始做某事
grown-up adj.成年的=adult n.成年人=adult(复数:grown-ups=adults)
24. care for照顾=look after=take care of；喜欢
care v/n.关心；照顾；在乎
careful adj.认真的；仔细的 carefully adv.认真地；仔细地 carefulness n.仔细
careless adj.粗心的 carelessly adv.粗心地 carelessness n.粗心
be careful=watch/look out小心 be careful of/about/with sth小心某物
be careful (not) to do sth小心（不）做某事
care about关心 take great care非常小心

26. run free四处走动（此处free是副词）

run out of=use up用完（run out用完，但是后面不加宾语,run out of加宾语）

v.平躺（过去式/过去分词lay-lain) lie down躺下 lie in bed躺在床上
n.谎言（复数：lies) tell a lie=tell lies撒谎
